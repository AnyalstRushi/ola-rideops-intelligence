INTERPRETATION GUARDRAIL

Unfulfilled is not lost revenue.

Cancelled bookings and driver-not-found records are not completed revenue. A success status also does not, on its own, establish settled revenue after refunds or incomplete-ride checks.

Use these snapshot values as descriptive evidence—not as a forecast, causal claim, or audited financial statement.
